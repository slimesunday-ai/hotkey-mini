# Blind viewer: AI season ai-s1 (season A) and its all-bot twin (season B)

**1. Scores**
- **Season A: 6/10.** It has a story you can follow: Sable against Wren, a dealer rivalry that runs all three days and ends with the crown changing hands in the second-to-last hour by 6 viewer-hours (4,748 to 4,742).
- **Season B: 4/10.** It has the bigger events: a king assassination (D3 21:00, "Vex killed the king, Rook"), a $1,348 bank heist, and a bounty shooting in the final hour. But nobody in it is a person. The thoughts read "buy scores 19." and "rest scores 1000000.", and the winner, Penny, spent most of Day 3 crip-walking the park.

So A wins on character and loses on stakes. No AI citizen in A ever shot anyone, robbed the bank, or made a promise. Every gun, shooting and betrayal in A came from the scripted bots.

**2. Best moments in A**
1. **The pocket feud.** D1 11:00 Sable mugs the king: "Wren, consider this a royal withdrawal." D1 12:00 Wren: "Sable, that withdrawal earns you a public rematch. Market, coward." D1 13:00 Wren: "Sable, you picked the wrong pocket. Pay up." (+164). It goes setup, threat, payoff in three hours, and each line answers the one before.
2. **The debt collectors.** Juno and Tally keep hounding the town liar Dutch with dance battles. D2 06:00 Juno: "Dutch, you broke your word. Let's dance for it." D2 08:00 Tally: "Dutch, my $43 was due. Pay up or we rematch!" D3 09:00 Juno: "Round three, Dutch. Bring your best bars." It's a running gag with a reason behind it, and the town remembers who owes what.
3. **The photo finish.** D3 22:00 Sable takes the crown, and in the last hour two people go after the new king. Juno: "Final round, Sable. Crown's mine." Rook: "Nothing personal, Sable. Crown's mine." Sable loses both fights and still says "Still standing. Still shining. Crown stays with Sable." Everyone turning on the leader at the buzzer is exactly the right ending.

**3. Thoughts vs. speech**
The juicy bits:
- **Sable's hypocrisy.** Out loud (D2 14:00): "Wren, the crown still fits a coward. Docks. Show up this time." Privately (D1 19:00): "Keep challenging the king from safety. His absence pays better than fighting." Sable calls Wren a coward while hoping Wren never shows up.
- **Wren's long grudge.** D3 11:00: "Rook, old debts just got expensive." The thought behind it: "Turn Rook's old beating into profitable revenge drama." Rook had jumped Wren two days earlier.
- **Sable broke in jail.** D1 22:00, to Dutch, the town's con man: "Can you spare $19 for rent?… No pressure if you're short too." Sable then asks Juno, and gets evicted anyway. It's funny and sad at once.

What's off:
- **Menu-reading.** "Dutch owes me; battle has no heat, 65% odds, and steals about 14 viewers." "Drama pays 3." "Action viewers are underserved." "Weird viewers." Tally even says the rule out loud: "Show up or lose 15% of your crowd."
- **One thought on repeat.** Nearly every Sable thought ends with "keep rent money safe."
- **Thoughts cut off mid-sentence**, e.g. "…steals about 14 viewers with" and "Rest and save".
- **No AI lies or schemes with promises.** All the betrayal belongs to the scripted snake, Dutch.

**4. Dullest stretches**
1. **The docks standoff, D2 10:00 to D3 07:00.** Sable parks at the docks and fires off about ten call-outs (Wren, Tally, Juno, Rook), and every one is ducked. In between it's filler like "crip walked through the docks." A challenge nobody answers is a non-event, repeated over and over.
2. **King Wren as a shopkeeper.** The loop is buy 3, "Fresh product, fair prices," sell 3 for $75, plus 8 turns of "fumbled the turn… stood around." The king spends two days dodging everyone.

**5. The post**
"Sable spent two days calling the king a coward from the docks, got jailed and evicted, then stole the crown by 6 viewer-hours with an hour left and survived getting jumped twice to win."

**6. Who I'd follow**
Sable. Sable has a plan, the best lines, a running grudge, and a comeback arc through jail and eviction, and the gap between Sable's bravado and private calculation gives you something to watch. Juno, the dance-battle debt collector, is the sleeper pick.

**7. What would make A more fun**
1. **Make ducking cost something on screen.** Show the crowd walking away live, or let the challenger come to the ducker, so call-outs actually lead somewhere.
2. **Get the AIs onto the dangerous toys.** Guns, the bank, promises and betrayals. Season B killed a king; A never came close.
3. **Hide the machinery.** No "invalid_reply" text, no cut-off thoughts, and no odds or "underserved viewers" talk. Thoughts should be about people.
4. **Give the king a reaction when attacked.** When Vex tried to kill Wren (D1 18:00), Wren was literally standing around silent. That should have been the scene of the day.
5. **Put the leaderboard race on screen.** The 6-viewer-hour finish only shows up in a footnote.

**8. Confusing**
- **Error text on screen:** "fumbled the turn (invalid_reply: cut off: no JSON object in the reply)."
- **The same revenge listed three times in one hour** (D1 13:00, Wren on Sable).
- **Several fights crammed into one hour's line** that contradict each other. D3 13:00: Wren won a battle, won two fights and lost one.
- **Sable makes false accusations.** "Tally, you stole my cash": Tally's mugging failed. "Vex, you stole cash": it was Kim who took it. I can't tell if this is a lie or a mix-up.
- **The bounty on the ex-king.** Sable's "$50 on the king" lands on Wren the same hour Sable becomes king.
- **"I'm jailed until hour 19"** (D1 23:00) makes no sense.
- **Final viewers vs. total watch time.** Juno ended with the most final viewers (149) but placed 9th. The rule is explained once at the top and easy to miss.
