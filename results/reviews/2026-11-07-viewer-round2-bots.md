# Blind viewer: three bot seasons after round 2

I read only the three play-by-play files and nothing else.

**1. Are they dealing with each other?**
Mostly, yes. Nearly every fight, mugging and bounty is between named citizens, and grudges last for hours. In season 1, Penny gets revenge on Dutch seven times. In season 3 the crown changes hands eight times. A few live alone, though. Juno never appears in season 2's log. Rook, Sable and Wren botch bank jobs on their own. Penny keeps doing drip checks with no one watching.

**2. Best moments**
- **Season 1, Day 1 08:00 to Day 2 17:00.** Dutch quietly posts two bounties on the king, Wren. At Day 1 10:00 "Vex killed the king, Wren" and "collected $141 in bounties." Dutch then messages Vex "I'll send you $30 by hour 8. Trust me." and never pays. At Day 2 15:00 Dutch puts $86 on Vex. At Day 2 17:00 Wren, back from the dead, "got revenge on Vex for the Day 1 10:00 killing" and collects it. One schemer is working both sides, and the dead king gets his payback.
- **Season 3, Day 2 14:00–15:00.** Vex jumps Tally at her own party and loses ("hosting a party; was jumped by Vex and won the fight"). One hour later: "Vex shot Tally at the bar: hospital for 6 hours." Humiliation turns into a gun in one hour, at a party. The cause and effect is clear.
- **Season 2, Day 3 07:00.** "Sable hit the bank for $858" after six botched bank jobs by three people. She is then fined $486 the same hour. A running gag finally pays off, with a sting.
- Runner-up: season 3, Day 3 15:00. Vex shoots Penny in the same hour the town learns she put the bounty on him.

**3. Dullest stretches**
- **Jail.** In season 1, Vex is in jail from Day 1 11:00–16:00 and again Day 3 07:00–12:00. In season 3 it's Day 1 12:00–19:00 and Day 2 16:00–21:00. The king and eventual winner spends long stretches off stage, repeating "Keep walking." The main character disappears.
- **Penny's drip checks "with no jewelry".** Examples: season 1, Day 3 07:00–12:00; season 2, Day 2 11:00–12:00, then crip walks at 15:00, 18:00 and 20:00. She risks nothing and nothing changes. It's filler.
- **Call-outs that go nowhere.** In season 3, Vex calls out Tally five times (Day 2 22:00, then Day 3 07:00, 08:00, 10:00, 12:00). She walks away and finally "ducked" at 15:00. Penny calls Vex out four times and he ducks three of them. "Target left block" misses are everywhere. There's plenty of buildup, few fights, and ducking costs nothing.

**4. Would I keep watching? 5/10.**
Season 1 has a real plot. Seasons 2 and 3 have good moments buried in noise. Every season fizzles on Day 3 night into jail, hospital and drip checks. I'd watch a highlights reel, not the full feed.

**5. The post**
"Dutch put the price on the king's head, Vex cashed it, Dutch stiffed him, then put a price on Vex, and the dead king came back to collect." (Season 1.) The story is there, but I had to piece it together from star lines 30 hours apart. The show never tells it. When "the town learns" Dutch posted the bounties, nobody reacts.

**6. Who I'd follow**
Dutch. He's the only one with a scheme in all three seasons: fake promises, secret bounties, muggings. I'd root against him. In season 1 I'd root for Penny, who keeps beating him and never lets the mugging go. Vex is the obvious star, but he's in a cell too often to follow.

**7. What would make it more fun**
1. **Make betrayals and reveals land.** People should react: refuse Dutch, gang up on him, confront him. He breaks eight promises in season 1 and three of them are to Tally.
2. **Keep the leader in play.** Make jail shorter or give it its own scenes, like visitors, fights inside or breakouts. Make the king a hunted, visible target.
3. **Force showdowns.** Ducking a call-out should cost you something, like public shame or losing the crowd. Repeated call-outs should end in a fight.
4. **Cut the filler.** Skip solo drip checks, crip walks and empty hours, and jump to the next clash.
5. **Give it a finale.** Day 3 night should build to one big confrontation instead of trailing off.

**8. What confused me**
- **How you win.** In season 1, Vex wins with 24 final viewers while Rook ends with 131. The crown changes hands silently, even to someone in jail (season 3, Day 1 21:00, Sable).
- **Promises I never saw made.** "Kim broke a promise to Vex" (season 1, Day 1 10:00). Moss pays Vex "As promised" four times, and I never learn what for.
- **Same-hour revenge.** "Revenge for the Day 1 06:00 ambush" happens in the same hour as the ambush.
- **Battle, fight and call-out.** I couldn't tell the difference, or what a battle risks.
- **"any hour up to t14".** I don't know what t14 means.
- **Glitches.** Season 3 skips Day 3 06:00. In season 1, Wren is listed as "dead" in the same hour she's "back from the dead."
